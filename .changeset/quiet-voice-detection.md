Calls now watch the microphone level apart from speech recognition. The assistant pauses within about a tenth of a second when the visitor talks, even softly, and even before any words are recognised, so it can be interrupted while it greets. Add `call.voiceDetection` (on by default), `call.correct` to fix common mishearings, and `client.getMicLevel()` for level meters.
