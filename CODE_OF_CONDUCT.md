# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, and give feedback about the code, not the
person. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately (see `SECURITY.md` for the contact
channel). Every report is reviewed and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content or block
participants who break these rules.
